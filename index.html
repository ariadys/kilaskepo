Kilas Kepo

Kilas Kepo is an Indonesian-language content account that publishes short, sourced explainers about interesting facts and trending news on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

About the Kilas Kepo Publisher app

The Kilas Kepo Publisher is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Kilas Kepo accounts. It prepares image carousels and short vertical videos from the owner's own content and posts them to the owner's own social media accounts on a schedule.

On TikTok, the app uses Login Kit so the owner can authorize the app, and the Content Posting API to upload the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account. It does not access, collect, or post on behalf of any other TikTok users.
